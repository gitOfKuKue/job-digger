# Job Digger

Job Digger finds contact details for people in a target job role, then lets you reach out from the same system.

## What it does

- Look up people by job position (title, role, or similar targeting).
- Surface a connectable contact — typically an email, and other usable contact methods when available.
- Send email to that contact without leaving the app.

## Why it exists

Hiring, sales, and recruiting often start with a role, not a name. This project is built so you can go from “who holds this job?” to “I can email them” in one place.
